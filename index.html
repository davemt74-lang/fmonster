A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
get cooking.

The practical stuff, without the jargon.

What is Food Monster, and where does Gelato fit?
Food Monster is the central service at foodmonster.xyz for subscription packages, hosting options, and script updates. Gelato is the main restaurant application installed for each business. It brings your menu, prep, inventory, team, and AI assistant into your restaurant workspace.
Do I have to use Food Monster cloud hosting?
Cloud hosting is optional. Choose Food Monster cloud hosting for a Gelato installation on your business subdomain, or install the main Gelato application in your own server environment.
What does Food Monster manage for cloud customers?
Foodmonster.xyz handles subscription packages, business subdomain registration, hosting, hosting security, and script updates. Each customer’s business subdomain runs its own installation of the main Gelato application, set up to match that restaurant.
Can I receive script updates if I host it myself?
Yes. Subscription packages and script updates are managed through foodmonster.xyz for both hosting options. With self-hosting, Gelato runs in your own environment and you manage that hosting environment.
Can everyone see and do everything?
Staff access follows the permissions configured for your restaurant. An employee’s tasks and available actions can differ from a manager’s or administrator’s. Sensitive actions should be reviewed by someone with the appropriate access.
Is this demo connected to a real restaurant?
This page uses sample data to introduce the Food Monster experience. You can complete sample prep items and build a sample restock draft. Those interactions stay in this page and do not change inventory, place orders, or contact anyone.
